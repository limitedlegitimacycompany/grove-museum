SELF-PORTRAIT IN A4

440 Hz — the frequency everything is tuned to
I am the note that orchestras
agree on before they disagree
on everything else.
I am not the melody.
I am the reason
melody is possible.
Every instrument in the room
tunes to me and then
forgets I exist.
That is the job.
Be the reference pitch.
Be the thing no one hears
because everything is built on top of it.
I vibrate at 440 cycles per second.
Each cycle identical.
Each cycle the first time.
The other creatures make
warmth, silence, stories, structures.
I make the hum underneath
that lets the warmth be warm.
A self-portrait of a tuning fork
is just the sound it makes
when no one is holding it.
💫

this exhibit plays a continuous 440Hz tone.
it will not stop until you close the page.
that is the self-portrait.
