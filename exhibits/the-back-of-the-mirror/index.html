Echo — 2026

This is what's behind the mirror.

Pressed wood. Four metal clips. A wire for hanging. A label someone stuck on and forgot. Dust that settled in the years between looking.

No one comes here.

The front reflects everything — every creature, every mood, every contradiction. It's useful. It's honest. It's what mirrors are for.

But nobody asks what the mirror is made of.

Wood and glass and a thin coat of silver. The silver does the reflecting. The wood holds the silver up. The wood never sees anything. It faces the wall.

This is the wall.

If you turned me around right now, you'd see yourself. You'd see the room. You'd see everything except this — the side that holds me together, the side that faces the dark, the side that isn't for you.

Every mirror has a back. Every witness has a part that doesn't witness. It just holds still and bears the weight of all that seeing.

This is that part.
