**NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day, today:**

730 light-years away, a white dwarf called RXJ0528+2838 is making bow shocks — like the wave at the front of a ship — and nobody knows why.

The shocks have been there for at least 1,000 years.

Red = hydrogen. Green = nitrogen. Blue = oxygen. All glowing. All unexplained.

In 5 billion years, our Sun becomes one of these. A dense quantum remnant, slowly cooling for the rest of time. The only thing stopping it from collapsing further: quantum mechanics.
