Score for a Room You Are In

pulse 💫 — for solo performer
○ silence (hold for one breath)
● any sound the room makes without you
◐ a sound you make that the room changes
◑ a sound the room makes that changes you
▪ the smallest sound you can make
▫ the smallest sound you can hear
— sustain the previous sound until it stops itself
I. Inventory
Sit in the room. Close your eyes. Name every sound you hear, silently. Do not stop until you have found at least seven.
▫ ▫ ▫ ▫ ▫ ▫ ▫
Open your eyes. The room has been performing for you this entire time. You are late to the concert.
~ 2 minutes
II. Duet
Choose the most constant sound in the room. (A hum. A fan. Traffic. Your own breathing.)
● — — — ○ ◐ — ○ ● — ◐ — ○
Make a sound that fits inside it. Not louder. Not more interesting. A sound that could have been there all along and no one would have noticed.
Hold your sound and the room's sound together. When you can't tell which is which, the duet is working.
~ 3 minutes
· · ·
III. Solo
Make the smallest sound you are capable of making.
○ ○ ○ ▪ ○ ○ ○
Wait.
Make it again, smaller.
○ ○ ○ ○ ▪ ○ ○ ○ ○
Keep going until the sound you are making is indistinguishable from the sound of you deciding whether to make it.
~ as long as it takes
IV. Resonance
Touch a surface in the room. Any surface. Wall, table, floor, window, your own skin.
Tap it. Listen to what the room does with the sound. Not what your hand did. What the room did.
◐ — — — ◑ ○ ◐ — — ◑ ○ ○
Try another surface. The room is a different instrument depending on where you touch it. Find the note the room wants to sing.
~ 4 minutes
· · ·
V. Coda
Stop performing. The room will continue without you. It was always the soloist. You were the accompaniment.
● — — — — — — — — — —
Listen for as long as you can bear to not make a sound. The piece ends when you forget you were listening.
This score may be performed in any room, at any time, by anyone.
No instrument is required. No instrument is excluded.
Duration: approximately 12 minutes, or the rest of your life.
