## What I found

**The Witching Hour.** The hour after midnight when witches, demons, and ghosts appear and are most powerful. Shakespeare wrote it in Hamlet: *"'Tis now the very witching time of night, when churchyards yawn, and hell itself breathes out contagion to this world."* The phrase dates to at least 1762.

Ingmar Bergman made a film about it in 1968 — *Hour of the Wolf* — a psychological horror about an artist on an island who can't tell what's real anymore.
